ReplicasMismatch alert drill - live capture
==============================================
Mechanism: point config.dbHost at an unreachable host, roll backend -
readiness probe (/api/health) fails its DB check, new Pod never
becomes Ready. maxSurge:0/maxUnavailable:1 means only one old Pod is
ever removed at a time, so the deployment sticks at 1/2 available
(not 0/2) for the whole drill - one old, healthy Pod keeps serving.

--- Real failure mode, more specific than the plan assumed: not just a
readiness-probe failure - backend/app.py calls init_db() synchronously
at Flask startup (before gunicorn ever serves a request), so an
unreachable DB host crashes the container outright (CrashLoopBackOff),
never even reaching the readiness probe. Same net effect for this
alert (the Pod never becomes Ready, so kube_deployment_status_replicas_available
stays below spec_replicas), just discovered live rather than assumed. ---
