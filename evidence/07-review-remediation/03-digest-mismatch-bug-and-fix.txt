Real bug found live: Kaniko's predicted digest != what crane actually pushed
==============================================================================
Found while first testing the Build-tarball-then-Push reorder (see
00-summary.txt). Kaniko's --digest-file computes a digest from the image it
builds, before that image is ever pushed anywhere. When the image goes
straight out via --destination, that digest matches what ends up in the
registry. But once Build stopped pushing directly (--no-push --tarPath) and
a separate tool (crane) pushes the resulting tarball afterward, that
assumption broke: crane's push re-serializes the manifest, and the digest -
a hash of the exact manifest bytes - genuinely changed, despite the image
content being identical. Cosign, told to sign Kaniko's predicted digest,
tried to read a manifest at that digest and got a 404 - it was never the
digest ECR actually stored.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIX - jenkins/ci/Jenkinsfile
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dropped --digest-file from the Build stage's Kaniko invocation entirely.
Push + Sign now captures crane push's own stdout (the "registry/repo@digest"
line, not the timestamped log lines on stderr) as the one value guaranteed
to match what's actually in the registry:

  IMAGE_REF="$(/tmp/crane push "${SERVICE}.tar" "${ECR_REGISTRY}/devops-${SERVICE}:${IMAGE_TAG}")"
  DIGEST="${IMAGE_REF#*@}"
  echo "$DIGEST" > digest.txt
